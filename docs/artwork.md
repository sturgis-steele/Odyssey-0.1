# Console artwork

The opening screen's decorative terrain plate is stored at `frontend/src/assets/terrain.webp`. The built-in ImageGen tool generated the illustration during development. The application serves the encoded image from its local bundle.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a wide, text-free panoramic plate for the opening screen of an offline field computer called Odyssey. On the right half, an isolated rugged alpine ridgeline, in three-quarter perspective, rendered as an exquisite scientific terrain survey with dense, fine copper-orange contour lines and point-cloud stippling over deep graphite shaded relief. Low foreground foothills, one distinctive asymmetrical peak. The relief gradually dissolves into a fine dark field-chart grid. On the left half leave near-black charcoal negative space for GUI copy. Quiet, precise, tactile analog cartography. Near-black charcoal background, burnt amber and warm muted ivory highlights only. Wide 3:1 composition, no lettering, no numbers, no logos, no UI, no border.
